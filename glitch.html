glitch art

the exploration of glitches to intentionally alter an image

My project utilizes various paintings from different eras. My first piece uses an image of "The Kiss" by Gustav Klimt, painted during the Vienna Secession. The next piece utilizes an image of the "Mona Lisa", painted by Leonardo da Vinci during the High Renaissance. I used text data bending to create the glitch effect on both images, which was more unpredictable to create and required lots of trial and error. The bottom piece uses "The Girl with a Pearl Earring" by Johannes Vermeer during the Baroque period. I used Audacity databending to create the glitch effect on that one. The significance of using all of the pieces from different eras of art is to signify the growing influence of technology on art, especially in the form of generative AI. This harms all artists and diminishes the significance of art in culture and of those who paved the way through different periods of time. The glitch on each painting represents how technology is diminishing the humanity in art.
