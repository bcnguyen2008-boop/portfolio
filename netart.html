net art

the use of the web to create art

I made a little 'Raise a Pooh' simulator where you take care of Pooh! My main idea was to create a cozy game-like navigation system where you could make choices about how you wanted to take care of Pooh. Its inspired by a lot of roblox games i've seen where you take care of different creatures or characters. The links contribute to the meaning of the game since they allow the viewer to choose their own path, as if they're raising Pooh on their own! A technical challenge that I faced was the images. Since most of the Pooh images had originally white backgrounds, I had to manually remove each background and convert it into a PNG in order for it to work. I also struggled with placing the buttons at the right locations because it could look weird if you go on a mobile device or minimize your window.
