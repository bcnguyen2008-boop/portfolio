remix

the exploration of how identity can be portrayed through digital media

I combined the "Why is he lying?" meme with Anakin Skywalker/Darth Vader. The original format with Wong's skeptical face and the LinkedIn page is meant to comment on how he is constantly used in Marvel projects, seemingly overworked, which is why his resume continues to grow. Using Anakin Skywalker and his future identity as Darth Vader as the LinkedIn profile changes the meaning from an exhausted character to someone who rebrands themselves in a drastically different way from their digital profile and who they really are. Using Anakin and Vader instead of two images of Anakin highlights the drastic shift in his identity. It emphasizes the text's meaning since Anakin has not yet become Vader, so his portrayal on the app is seen as a bluff and emphasizes the lie.
