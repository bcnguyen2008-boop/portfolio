digital identity

digital culture throught mashups and remixes of exisiting media

the exploration of glitches to intentionally alter an image

I am exploring how virtual avatars can embody people in the digital world. I chose to exaggerate the avatars in the piece by using multiple versions of them. I used Adobe Photoshop, and I employed various techniques such as masking, copying, and compositing. These processes exemplify the meaning of the work since it shows the collaboration of two different worlds, the digital and physical. This work functions as an exploration of digital identity because it shows how people can portray themselves digitally even in a physical setting. For me, the first thing that came to mind was ROBLOX because people take the time to customize their characters to express themselves. The significance of using my and my friends' avatars in a physical setting is to show how people have two versions of themselves, digital and real, and how it can be used to represent them without their actual faces.
